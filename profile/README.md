## 🌿 About BIKKSA Mutiara Hati

**BIKKSA Mutiara Hati is a digital consultation platform designed to make religious, family, and psychological support more accessible, structured, and compassionate.**

Developed for the *Biro Konsultasi Keluarga Sakinah 'Aisyiyah (BIKKSA)*, the platform aims to provide a welcoming space where individuals can express their concerns, understand their available support options, and connect with the appropriate consultation services.

More than simply bringing consultations online, BIKKSA Mutiara Hati is designed to support the entire service journey, from initial inquiries and needs assessment to scheduling, consultation, session summaries, follow-up, and referrals.

Guided by the values of empathy, trust, professionalism, and confidentiality, the platform seeks to help people access support without unnecessary complexity or uncertainty.

> **Mendengar • Memahami • Mendampingi**
>
> *Listening • Understanding • Supporting*

> 🚧 **BIKKSA Mutiara Hati is currently in development.**
>
> Its features, service workflows, and implementation will evolve through service validation, prototyping, testing, and evaluation with relevant stakeholders.

## 💛 The Need for Accessible Support

Traditional consultation processes may also involve fragmented scheduling, communication, documentation, and follow-up, making it harder to maintain a consistent service experience.

BIKKSA Mutiara Hati is being designed to address three key challenges:

## ✨ Our Approach

BIKKSA Mutiara Hati connects consultation access, service coordination, and ongoing support within a unified digital experience.

| Stage           | How It Works                                                                                                                 |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Explore**  | Users learn about available consultation services and identify the type of support they may need.                            |
| **2. Share**    | Users describe their concerns through an accessible initial assessment.                                                      |
| **3. Match**    | The system helps identify suitable support personnel based on service needs, relevant competencies, and availability.        |
| **4. Schedule** | Users select an available consultation time after the appropriate personnel have been assigned.                              |
| **5. Consult**  | Consultations take place through supported communication methods, including chat and planned video-call capabilities.        |
| **6. Reflect**  | Consultation summaries, recommendations, and relevant next steps are documented.                                             |
| **7. Continue** | Users can access their consultation history and relevant follow-up or referral information according to their access rights. |

This approach is intended to make the process easier to understand for users while supporting more coordinated service delivery for consultation personnel and administrators.

## 🕊️ Consultation Services

BIKKSA Mutiara Hati is designed around three primary consultation services.

### 📖 Religious Consultation

Religious guidance provided by qualified *Mubalighat 'Aisyiyah*, according to their areas of competence.

Potential topics include:

* Islamic worship and everyday religious practices.
* Zakat and inheritance.
* Family-related Islamic jurisprudence.
* Other religious matters within the service's scope.

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Family Consultation

Family support provided by the **Family Support Team**, focusing on interpersonal relationships and family well-being.

Potential topics include:

* Marital and partner relationships.
* Family communication and conflict.
* Parenting and child development.
* Family dynamics and other relevant concerns.

### 🧠 Psychological Consultation

Psychological consultation provided by psychologists within their professional scope and competencies.

Potential topics include:

* Emotional well-being.
* Stress and anxiety.
* Personal and interpersonal concerns.
* Psychological education and appropriate follow-up support.

The platform is intended to help users identify an appropriate service without requiring them to understand professional classifications in advance.

**Important:** Initial assessments support service routing and the understanding of user needs. They are not intended to replace professional judgment or independently establish a psychological diagnosis.

## 🤝 Designed Around People

BIKKSA Mutiara Hati recognizes that every consultation involves individual circumstances, personal concerns, and different support needs.

The platform is designed to serve the following stakeholders:

**Clients and Users**

* Register and manage their profiles.
* Submit consultation requests and describe their concerns.
* Select available consultation schedules.
* Participate in consultations.
* Access permitted session summaries, recommendations, and consultation history.
* Receive relevant follow-up and referral information.

**Consultation Personnel**

* Manage consultation schedules and assigned cases.
* Access client information relevant to their responsibilities.
* Conduct consultations within their professional competencies.
* Document session summaries and recommendations.
* Arrange appropriate follow-up or referrals.

**Administrators and Coordinators**

* Manage consultation personnel, competencies, and availability.
* Coordinate assignments and scheduling.
* Monitor consultation status and service continuity.
* Manage educational content and user complaints.
* Review service statistics and aggregated reports.

**System Administrators**

* Manage system configuration, user roles, and high-level access permissions.
* Support system security, maintenance, and operational reliability.

Each role is designed with defined responsibilities and access boundaries to help protect users and support accountable service delivery.

## 🔐 Privacy, Confidentiality & Safety

For BIKKSA Mutiara Hati, privacy and confidentiality are fundamental requirements, not optional features.

Consultations may involve sensitive information about personal relationships, family circumstances, and psychological well-being. The platform is therefore designed around the following principles.

* **Privacy by Design:** Collect only the information necessary to provide the service.
* **Role-Based Access Control:** Restrict access to information according to assigned responsibilities and authorization.
* **Confidential Consultation Records:** Limit access to consultation notes and conversations to authorized parties.
* **Informed Consent:** Provide understandable information about the service and its privacy practices.
* **Secure Data Handling:** Apply appropriate authentication, data protection, audit logging, and backup measures.
* **Responsible Reporting:** Use aggregated or de-identified information for service-level reporting wherever appropriate.
* **Human-Guided Decisions:** Support service classification and routing without replacing professional judgment.

### Safety Comes First

Some situations require more than routine consultation. Concerns involving violence, immediate danger, child safety, self-harm, or medical emergencies need appropriate escalation and referral procedures.

BIKKSA Mutiara Hati is intended to support these pathways through an initial safety assessment and service-routing process. The relevant escalation criteria, response procedures, and referral contacts must be established with BIKKSA and qualified professionals before implementation and operational use.

The platform is designed to support professional care, not replace emergency services or professional intervention.

## 📱 A Simpler Digital Experience

BIKKSA Mutiara Hati is planned as a mobile-first consultation application, with Android as the initial platform priority according to the project requirements.

The user experience is guided by a simple principle:

> **As easy to use as WhatsApp, with the structure of a professional consultation service.**

Key design considerations include:

* Clear navigation and familiar language.
* Short, manageable forms instead of overwhelming questionnaires.
* A straightforward consultation-request and scheduling process.
* Readable text and accessible interface elements.
* Responsive performance under common network conditions.
* Clear information about privacy, consent, and available services.
* Appropriate access to consultation history and follow-up information.

The intention is to reduce the barriers to seeking support while maintaining the structure and accountability required for professional consultation services.

## 🔄 From Initial Request to Continued Support

The platform is designed to support more than a single consultation session.

A typical service journey includes:

**Consultation Request → Initial Assessment → Service Matching → Scheduling → Consultation → Session Summary → Follow-up or Referral**

Behind this journey, service coordination helps ensure that:

* Consultation assignments reflect the user's needs and the personnel's competencies.
* Scheduling considers personnel availability.
* Consultation records remain accessible according to authorized roles.
* Follow-up actions and referrals can be documented and monitored.
* Administrators can monitor service operations without unnecessary access to sensitive consultation content.

This integrated approach aims to make support more consistent while helping BIKKSA maintain service quality and continuity.

## 🛠️ Development Roadmap

BIKKSA Mutiara Hati follows a phased development approach that places service readiness, user experience, and safety alongside technical implementation.

| Phase                               | Focus                                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **1. Analysis & Service Design**    | Validate user needs, consultation workflows, professional competencies, access permissions, and safety procedures.                                     |
| **2. UI/UX & Prototyping**          | Design and test the interface, navigation, and consultation journey.                                                                                   |
| **3. Minimum Viable Product (MVP)** | Develop core capabilities, including authentication, consultation requests, service matching, scheduling, chat, session records, and basic dashboards. |
| **4. Pilot Testing**                | Evaluate the application with a limited group of users and consultation personnel.                                                                     |
| **5. Evaluation & Improvement**     | Review usability, privacy, security, service workflows, and referral procedures.                                                                       |
| **6. Further Development**          | Expand educational content, analytics, integrations, and platform capabilities as needed.                                                              |

### Feature Priorities

* **P0 — Essential:** Registration and login, consultation requests, initial assessment, service routing, scheduling, booking, chat, consultation records, history, and foundational access controls.
* **P1 — Important:** Referrals, follow-up, notifications, educational content, video calls, and expanded dashboards.
* **P2 — Future Development:** Advanced analytics, automated recommendations, external integrations, community features, and broader platform support.

Feature availability will depend on implementation progress and validation with the relevant stakeholders.

BIKKSA Mutiara Hati is being developed with the understanding that technology alone cannot deliver meaningful consultation services.

A dependable digital service requires the right combination of thoughtful design, competent consultation personnel, clear service procedures, responsible coordination, and strong protection of personal information.
